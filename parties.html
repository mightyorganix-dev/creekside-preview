Parties

Celebrate your child’s special day with horses, laughter, and unforgettable memories. Fun-filled pony parties for younger riders and guided trail ride parties for older kids, teens, and families.

Safe, well-trained horses, experienced staff, and a beautiful country setting. We handle the horses — you enjoy the smiles, photos, and fun.

Option 1: 2 Horse / Pony Party

Ages 8 & under
$400

Includes: 1 hour of riding taking turns — handlers leading horses or fun games on horseback; brushing and grooming; feeding horse treats.

  • Tables available for food, drinks, presents — stay as long as you like after your ride
  • We provide: 2 ponies or small horses, 2 handlers, helmets, grooming tools & instruction
  • No limit on number of riders (they share the 2 mounts during the hour)
  • Additional horses: $50 each

Trail Ride Parties — Ages 9 & up (adults welcome)

$65 per rider + tip

Mini-lesson plus trail ride — each participant rides approx. 50–60 minutes. Helmets provided and required. Great for birthdays, anniversaries, bridal showers, reunions, and more.
