Summer Riding Camp

Where horse lovers come to camp!

$450 per week

Ages 7 to 15 · Monday–Friday throughout June and July

Earlier drop-off available every week at no charge starting at 7:15 am (reservations required). Pick-up is 3 pm — no after-camp hours.

Most campers ride an average of 3 to 4 horses each day

1. Ride in 4 different scenarios

  1. Instructor-led riding lessons — for proper technique
  2. Hop-on-hop-off — try various horses & saddles
  3. Bareback riding — for balance
  4. Trail riding — shady, cool fun

2. Ride on different terrains

  1. Large sand arena
  2. Round pen / small arena
  3. Grass field
  4. Tree-lined nature trail

3. Learn safety around the barn

  • Leading & handling a horse from the ground
  • Horse & hoof care, grooming & tacking
  • How to lunge a horse
  • Scavenger hunts, arts & crafts about horses & behaviors

4. Our horses are experienced

  • Most have been in our camp program for over a decade
  • They live here year round — loving pets
  • They give riding lessons on weekends during the year

5. Beginner, intermediate & advanced riders

  • Lessons separated by age & experience
  • Beginners get extra assistance with Jr counselors on the ground
  • Team games geared to all levels (walk, trot, or canter)
  • Jump/canter lessons tested into by skill
  • Trail rides are beginner level & guided

Afternoons: horse bathing, feeding, untacking English & Western, hoof care, horse health lessons, water play & more.
