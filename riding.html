Trail Ride Lessons

Horseback riding near Nashville, Franklin, Thompson’s Station & Columbia, TN

Trail Ride Lessons

Option 1: 1-Hour Family Trail Ride

Scenic, wooded 50+ acre property. Narrow and wide trail sections, hills, valleys, and a photo shoot in a meadow clearing halfway. Lots of nature and easygoing fun for all.

Ages 8+
$65 per rider + tip

Perfect for families, first-time riders, and anyone looking for a shorter recreational ride.

Option 2: 2-Hour Customized Adventure Ride

Twice the time in the saddle, built around your group’s experience and comfort. Relaxed and scenic, or a little more challenge — including opportunity for faster gaits.

Ages 11+ · Best Value
$100 per rider + tip

A $30 savings per rider for double the time. Ideal if you loved the 1-hour ride and want more, or want trot & canter where appropriate.

What makes the Adventure Ride different

  • Customized to your group — guide discusses experience and goals before you mount
  • More of the property — farther trails, wooded paths, open fields, pond ridges
  • Time to connect with your horse
  • Small groups, experienced guides — max about 6 riders

What to expect

  1. Check-in: arrive about 15 minutes early to sign waivers and meet your guide
  2. Meet your horse — matched to size and experience
  3. Safety briefing and riding basics
  4. Hit the trail — guided, customized; photo opportunities
  5. Back at the barn — thank your horse and guide

Good to know

  • What to wear: Long pants and closed-toe shoes with a small heel (boots best). No sandals or sneakers with thick tread.
  • Helmets: Provided; required for riders under 18
  • Weight limit: 250 lbs for the comfort and safety of our horses
  • Weather: Rides may be rescheduled for heat, storms, or unsafe conditions. Call if unsure.
  • Reservations required. Weekends fill quickly.
Questions? Call or text 615-595-7547 or email creeksidestables@gmail.com. When booking, state Option 1 or 2 in the comments.
