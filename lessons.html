Leader in Training (LIT) — By Invitation Only

For our regular lesson students. Helps increase and fast-track a rider’s effectiveness, knowledge, and skills as an equestrian. Ages 10+ who want extra time around horses and are willing to work.

  • When: Lesson day — 7:30 am to approx. 3 pm
  • What: Assignments and projects demonstrating safety & understanding around horses
